Hello! My name is Jaime. I'm a software engineer and a researcher with a passion for the application of computing technologies to the arts. When I'm away from my various computing devices, I like to spend my time [baking](https://www.instagram.com/jaimesbread/). 

I've labelled this website a journal as I think of it as a collection of ideas that I have sporadically grouped together in a manner similar to how one might use a journal or a notebook. Whether they were expressed formally or informally, I wanted to have this space to record these thoughts in the hope that they might prove useful to someone--even if that someone were me in the future! Think of this as an active repository where I log my active projects, builds, and research. %% If anything sparks the urge for discussion, please reach out to me at%%
